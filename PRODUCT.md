# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Owners of established home-service companies (gutters, landscaping, remodeling, trades) in a single local market. They already have demand and a reputation; they suspect work is leaking somewhere and are skeptical of agencies that sell more advertising. They arrive from a referral, an ad or a search, usually on a phone between jobs.

## Product Purpose
marketingperformance.net sells Marketing Performance Group's demand-recovery work, delivered through EngageEngine™. The homepage has ONE job: get the owner to start the 12-question Demand Collapse Diagnostic™. Success = diagnostic starts. Everything else on the page (proof, the four failure moments, the one-company-per-market promise, the five-part plan, risk reversal) exists only to earn that click.

## Brand Personality
Direct, seasoned, exclusive. A 41-year veteran who says it plainly, works for one company per trade per market, and diagnoses before prescribing. Confident without hype; the exclusivity is the product, stated as fact, not as urgency.

## Anti-references
- Generic agency site: stock photos, "grow your business" headlines, logo walls.
- SaaS startup template: big-number stat rows, gradient blobs, identical feature-card grids.
- Hard-sell funnel page: countdown timers, fake scarcity, red arrows.

## Design Principles
- One primary action, repeated at most at top and bottom.
- Say each idea once. Repetition reads as a funnel, not a veteran.
- Proof over claims: named client results carry the argument.
- Robbie's own words and face, never stock.
- Copy rules: EngageEngine™ always with ™; US spelling; no em dashes.

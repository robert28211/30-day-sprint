## PHASE 1 — LEAD MAGNET SYSTEM

### PRIMARY: THE MARKET SNAPSHOT
**The hook:** Competitive threat framing (tested highest with Mark)
**The delivery:** EngageEngine pull — real buyer activity in their city + trade, 48-72hr window. People who visited 3+ sites and haven't called anyone. One page. Free. Delivered within 24 hours of a reply. No form.

```
HOOK: "One of your [city] competitors may already
  have access to this buyer data."

DELIVERY: One-page report. Real EngageEngine™ pull.
  Active buyers in their market. 48-72 hour window.
  People already looking who haven't called anyone.
  Competitive capture signal included.

CTA: "We only work with one [trade] per market.
  [City] has one open slot. If we don't find 2X
  our fee in recoverable revenue within 90 days —
  we work free. Reply and I'll pull it tonight."
```

**Salty Pretzel Score: 10/10** — Seeing real buyers immediately creates "who has access to this right now? My competitor?"

**CRITICAL:** The snapshot IS the product demo. Build the template before Day 1. It must feel like you already know their business. Generic = dead.

### SECONDARY: THE DIAGNOSTIC (/diagnostic/)
**Mid-funnel ONLY. Never use as cold entry point.**
Mark gave it 2/10 as a cold entry ("BuzzFeed for contractors").
Deploy AFTER the snapshot lands and they're convinced the problem is real.
Reframe: "The Snapshot shows what's in your market. The Diagnostic shows why you're not capturing it."
Remove any mention of a follow-up call from the page.

## PHASE 2 — CHANNEL STRATEGY

### PRIMARY: META CUSTOM AUDIENCES
**The unfair advantage:** EngageEngine builds the audience list (HVAC dealers in Nashville TN by business size) and uploads directly to Meta. No auction competition. CPL drops dramatically.

- **Ad objective:** Messages (not Traffic, not Lead Gen)
- **Why Messages:** Routes directly to a conversation. First reply = qualification. No landing page friction.
- **Budget:** $30-50/day per active market
- **Geographic strategy:** One campaign per market (Nashville HVAC, Denver Roofing, Phoenix Plumbing). Category exclusive = natural urgency in each market.
- **The pitch on every sales call:** *"I found you using EngageEngine. That's exactly what we'll do for your customers."*

### SECONDARY: COLD EMAIL TO SAME LIST
Same people, different channel, multi-touch. By the time they get the email they may have already seen the ad twice. You're not cold anymore.

### FUTURE (not yet):
LinkedIn content — once 5 clients and case studies exist. Compounds over time.

## PHASE 4 — LEAD GETTERS

### REFERRAL PROGRAM: THE OPEN SLOT NETWORK
**When:** Results review call at month 2-3. Not a text — a call.
**How:** Walk client through their numbers. They feel proud. Ask then.

```
REFERRAL ASK (live on results call):
"[Client name] — really glad the numbers are
working. Quick question. Do you know any other
business owners — different trade here in [city],
or [same trade] guys you know in other markets?

I only work with one company per market per trade,
so there's no conflict. If they're a fit I'd reach
out the same way I reached you. Anyone come to mind?"
```

**Then write the intro text TOGETHER on the call:**
```
Pre-written text the client forwards:
"Hey [Name] — I've been working with a guy
for a few months on our targeting. Not an agency,
different thing entirely — he finds buyers in your
market before they search. We've had a solid quarter.
He doesn't take two companies in the same market
so there's limited availability. His name is [Founder].
Worth a quick call. Want me to connect you?"
```

**Incentive:** One free month ($3,500 value) + named on client results page.
Note: Mark told us the incentive doesn't change his decision — the ease of referring does. Make the text so frictionless his peer doesn't feel ambushed.

### AFFILIATE PRIORITY ORDER (by 90-day probability)

**#1 — Google Ads agencies serving home service (no audience targeting)**
Do this first. This week if possible.
- They have your exact buyer
- A gap they can't fill (audience differentiation)
- Immediate complementary fit, zero competition
- Offer: $500 referral fee or white-label arrangement

**#2 — Business coaches (Tom Reber / Contractor Fight tier)**
- 10% of monthly fee, capped at 12 months ($4,200 max per referred client)
- One coach with 50 clients = 50 qualified warm conversations
- Timeline: 60-90 days to first referral

**#3 — Podcast guest (Owned & Operated, Contractor Fight)**
- Not for immediate leads — for the ARTIFACT
- One episode = URL you use in every sales conversation for 2 years
- George Rogerson story is the episode pitch

**SKIP until 40+ clients:**
- ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro (too small to matter to them yet)
- Equipment distributors (too slow)

### DELEGATION PLAN
- **First hire:** at 3 clients with documented process (not 5 — too late)
  - Role: Snapshot puller / outreach coordinator
  - $2,500-3,500/month
  - Must have QA checklist with interpretation flags, not just mechanical pulls
  - Red flags to document: seasonal anomalies, tracking issues, competitor moves
- **Second hire:** at 8-10 clients
  - Salesperson on performance: $2,000 base + $500/close
  - You handle close only

## PHASE 5 — RULE OF 100

### PRE-WORK (complete before Day 1 of the clock)
```
□ Domain warmed — minimum 2 weeks prior to first send
□ 3 market lists built, deduplicated, [X] fields populated
  with real numbers
□ Snapshot template built and tested on 2 real businesses
  (this is a sales asset, not a fulfillment task)
□ Airtable tracking setup — markets separated cleanly,
  reply/snapshot/call tracked per contact
□ Two subject line variants written (A/B from Day 1)
□ Meta campaigns loaded — one per market, blurred snapshot
  creative, Messages objective
□ Snapshot SLA policy scripted into reply template:
  "First 2 same-day. Remainder within 24hrs, communicated
  proactively."
```

### DAILY ROUTINE (3-4 hrs/day after setup; 5-7 hrs in weeks 1-2)
```
6:00-7:00am  Email send block
             100 contacts across active markets
             A/B subject lines (odd = A, even = B)

11:30am      Meta check + respond
             New conversations: respond same day
             Spend pacing check

1:00-2:30pm  Snapshot delivery
             Pull and send all pending snapshots
             Same-day SLA if before noon

5:00pm       15 min tracking — log everything
```

### THE HONEST MATH
```
100 emails/day × 1.5% reply = 1-2 snapshots/day
1-2 snapshots × 20% call rate = 2 calls/week
2 calls/week × 25% close = ~2 clients/month

Fully booked (15 clients): 7-8 months
Break-even: month 3-4

Month 1 revenue: $0-3,500
Month 3 revenue: $7,000-10,500
Month 6 revenue: $28,000-35,000
Month 8 revenue: $52,500 (fully booked)
```

### OPTIMIZATION TRIGGERS
```
Days 1-30:  Volume only. Hit 100/day. No optimization.
            EXCEPTION: A/B subject lines from Day 1.
            Everything else — leave alone.

Days 31-60: Fix the biggest drop-off.
            Target: double reply rate from baseline.

Days 61-90: If reply rate stable above 3%:
              Open 2 more markets
              Hand email sequence to first hire
            If reply rate still under 3%:
              Don't expand — rewrite the email first
```

### WEEK 4 MINIMUM VIABLE SIGNAL
```
If by end of week 4 you don't have:
  → 30%+ open rate (deliverability clean)
  → 1%+ reply rate (copy working)
  → 1 call booked

STOP. Diagnose before continuing volume.
Problem is one of: deliverability, subject line,
or snapshot quality. Fix one variable at a time.
```

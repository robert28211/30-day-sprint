## MESSAGING RULES (don't break these)

- Never say "leads" or "lead generation"
- Never say "intent data" — say "buyer data" or "active buyers"
- Never say "targeting" without the "exclusive" modifier
- Always end with the guarantee — it's the conversion accelerant
- "EngageEngine™" always with the trademark symbol
- One slot per market framing on every ad — creates urgency without being pushy

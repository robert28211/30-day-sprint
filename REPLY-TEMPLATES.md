# Reply Templates & SLA Scripts
## $100M Leads Engine — Marketing Performance Group

---

## SLA POLICY (know this cold)

- **First 2 replies same day** — no matter what time they come in
- **All remaining replies:** within 24 hours, with a proactive heads-up if it'll be longer
- **Snapshot delivery:** same day if replied before noon, next morning if after noon
- **Human follow-up:** within 4 hours of any reply at any point in the sequence
- **Day 14 = last email. Never send after it.**

## QUICK REFERENCE — SEND RULES

| Situation | Response time |
|---|---|
| Any reply (email or Meta DM) | Within 4 hours |
| Snapshot requested before noon | Same day delivery |
| Snapshot requested after noon | Next morning |
| No response to snapshot after 3 days | Send Template 3 |
| Sequence Email 4 (Day 14) sent | That's the last one. Done. |
| They reply at any point in sequence | Pause sequence immediately |
